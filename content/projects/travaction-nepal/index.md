TravAction Nepal has been placing volunteers in Nepal communities since 2024, no international middlemen, no inflated program fees, no detached coordinators halfway across the world.

We're a locally-run Grassroot organization based in Kathmandu. Our team knows these schools, these families, and these projects personally. When you volunteer with us, your time and money stay where they belong: on the ground, in Nepal.
